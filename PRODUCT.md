# Product

## What this is

Clean Tablet is a reversible, no-root cleanup toolkit and a lightweight replacement Home app for
older Amazon Fire tablets. It makes the device feel calm, familiar, and fast without replacing the
firmware or removing the stock recovery path.

## Who it serves

It is for people reusing a low-memory Fire tablet who want their own apps to be the focus and do not
want Amazon promotions, duplicated services, or a visually busy launcher.

## Core job

Back up the current tablet state, disable only explicitly approved non-essential packages, install a
small launcher, and retain a tested route back to the stock Home experience.

## Design direction

The launcher uses a calm, iPad-inspired visual language: wallpaper-forward surfaces, generously
spaced icons, a concise search control, and a favourites dock. It remains recognisably Android and
does not copy Apple artwork or controls.

## Constraints

- Runs on Android API 22 and later, including Fire OS 7 on a 1 GB Fire 7 (2019).
- No root, bootloader changes, firmware flashing, analytics, Internet permission, background service,
  polling, blur, or video wallpaper.
- Every package change is reversible. Amazon Fire Launcher stays installed as a recovery Home.
- Touch targets are at least 48dp and portrait and landscape both remain usable.
